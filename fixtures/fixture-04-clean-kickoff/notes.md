# Project Kickoff Meeting Notes — Riverside Trail Segment

Attending: A. Rivera, M. Chen, and the parks department liaison.

## Purpose

The committee agreed to extend the Riverside Trail by 1.2 miles from the Main Street
bridge to the mill ruins, using the existing abandoned rail bed.

## Decisions

- The committee decided to use the abandoned rail bed alignment because it avoids
  two private parcels and already has a compacted base.

## Prerequisites

- The project needs an easement from the railroad holding company before any design
  work can start.
- A wetland delineation must be completed before construction permits are filed.
- The parks department requires a signed maintenance agreement before it will accept
  the trail dedication.

## Plan

- First, the committee will request the easement application packet.
- Next, the committee will commission the wetland delineation.
- Then, the committee will draft the maintenance agreement for the parks department.
- After those are approved, the committee will solicit construction bids.

## Constraints

- The grant budget is fixed at $180,000 and cannot absorb overruns.
- All work must finish before the October festival season begins.
