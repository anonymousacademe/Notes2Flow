# Problem

Stormwater flooding in the Maple Street neighborhood has gotten worse over the last
five years. Three of the last four October storms produced street flooding that
damaged basements on the 400 block. The city's storm drains were built in 1958 and
are undersized for current rainfall intensity.

# Constraints

- The budget for the pilot is $250,000 from the resilience fund.
- Any solution must be maintainable by the current public works crew.
- Work cannot block the school route during the academic year.

# Options

1. **Green infrastructure**: permeable pavement on the 400 block plus two bioswales.
   The city's 2024 green infrastructure pilot reduced peak runoff by 30% on a
   comparable street, which supports this option.
2. **Pipe upsizing**: replace the 1958 drains under Maple Street with larger culverts.
   This requires a lane closure permit and heavy equipment the crew does not own.

# Decision

We propose the green infrastructure option because it fits the budget and requires
no new equipment. The permeable pavement requires a compaction-tested subgrade;
the bioswales require city landscaping sign-off before construction.
The pipe upsizing option contradicts the budget constraint: engineering estimates
for culvert replacement exceed $600,000.
